Privacy Policy

OhMyCash! · last updated 23 August 2026

OhMyCash! collects nothing. There are no accounts, no servers, and no network requests. Everything you type stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer has no access to your data and no way to obtain it. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores, and where

The expenses, categories, accounts, budgets and currency settings you create are saved in the app's own storage on your device, using Apple's SwiftData. The same applies to what the category suggester learns from your entries. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

This data is included in your iPhone's normal iCloud or computer backups, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy rather than this one. You can also export a copy yourself at any time from Settings.

Category suggestions

When you type an expense, the app suggests a category. This runs entirely on your device, using Apple's Natural Language framework together with a word-counting model written into the app. No text is sent off the device, and no AI or cloud service is involved at any point.

Permissions

The app requests one optional permission: Face ID or Touch ID, and only if you switch on the app lock in Settings. It is off by default. The app never asks for location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone or notifications.

Third parties

There are none. No third-party SDKs, no analytics providers, no payment processors, no data brokers.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete individual expenses inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Because nothing ever leaves the device, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
